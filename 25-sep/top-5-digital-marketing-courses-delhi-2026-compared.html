SECTION 02Top 5 digital marketing courses in Delhi — compared

Here's a comparison of the top 5 digital marketing courses in Delhi for 2026:

1. Shaheed Sukhdev College of Business Studies (DU) — Certificate Course

A 100-hour weekend certificate program from Delhi University. Covers digital marketing fundamentals with practical training. Total fees: ₹20,200 (course fee ₹20,000 + application fee ₹200)[citation:2][citation:17].

Best for: Students wanting an affordable DU certificate alongside their degree. Duration: 3-4 months.

2. Hansraj College (DU) — Certificate Course

A six-month professional certificate program from Delhi University. Total fees: approximately ₹68,000[citation:1].

Best for: Students wanting a longer DU certificate with more depth. Duration: 6 months.

3. IIDE (Indian Institute of Digital Education) — PG Program

A full-time 11-month PG Diploma program with on-campus learning in Delhi. Total fees: ₹6.65 lakhs[citation:12].

Best for: Graduates wanting an intensive, placement-focused program. Duration: 11 months.

4. Digiperform — Classroom Course

A classroom-based digital marketing course with multiple branches across Delhi. Fees: ₹21,000[citation:1].

Best for: Learners wanting affordable classroom training with local presence. Duration: 3-4 months.

5. Simplilearn — Online Program

Online digital marketing courses with global certification. Fees range from ₹49,999 to ₹1,01,999 depending on the program[citation:5][citation:20].

Best for: Working professionals wanting flexible, online learning with global certification. Duration: 5-8 months.
Pro tip: College certificates are affordable but offer limited placement support. Private institutes cost more but provide hands-on training and job assistance. Online platforms offer flexibility at a premium.

SECTION 06How to choose the right course for you

Use this framework to decide:

Choose College Certificate If:

  • You want an affordable option
  • You're a student building skills alongside a degree
  • You don't need placement support
  • You want DU brand recognition
  • Budget is under ₹25,000

Choose Private Institute If:

  • You want hands-on training with live projects
  • You need placement support
  • You prefer classroom interaction
  • You want local job connections
  • Budget is ₹20,000-7,00,000

Choose Online Platform If:

  • You need flexible scheduling
  • You want global certification
  • You're a working professional
  • You prefer self-paced learning
  • Budget is ₹50,000-1,00,000

Key Factors to Consider:

  • Curriculum depth (SEO, Ads, Analytics, AI)
  • Hands-on projects and live campaigns
  • Placement support and hiring partners

  • Batch size and instructor access

SECTION 08Frequently asked questions

Which is the best digital marketing course in Delhi?

It depends on your goals. Shaheed Sukhdev College offers an affordable DU certificate (₹20,200). IIDE offers an intensive PG program (₹6.65 lakhs) with placement. Digiperform offers classroom training (₹21,000). Simplilearn offers online global certification (₹49,999+).

What is the fee range for digital marketing courses in Delhi?

Fees range from ₹20,200 (DU college certificate) to ₹6.65 lakhs (IIDE PG program). Private institutes like Digiperform charge around ₹21,000, while online platforms like Simplilearn charge ₹49,999 to ₹1,01,999.

Do digital marketing courses in Delhi provide placement support?

Private institutes like IIDE and Digiperform provide placement support. DU college certificates typically do not include placement. Online platforms like Simplilearn offer job assistance but it is less personalized.

Which certifications are valuable for digital marketing?

Google Ads certifications, HubSpot certifications, and Meta certifications are the most recognized. These are free or low-cost and can be taken independently of any course[citation:6].

How long does it take to complete a digital marketing course in Delhi?

Course durations range from 3-4 months (DU certificate, Digiperform) to 6 months (Hansraj) to 11 months (IIDE PG program). Online courses typically take 5-8 months.
